The NZ Welcome Box

Everything you wish you had packed, waiting in your room when you arrive. NZ$79 as an add-on to any package.

  • NZ$79 add-on to any package
  • Vegetarian by default, Jain on request
  • Waiting in your room on night one

What's inside

  • NZ power adapter (Type I)
  • Pre-registered SIM or eSIM instructions
  • Toothbrush, toothpaste, soap, shampoo
  • Masala chai, biscuits and namkeen
  • Ready-to-eat vegetarian meals for night one
  • Reusable water bottle
  • AT HOP card guide and a city map
  • Bank & IRD document checklist
  • Emergency numbers and your host's contact card
  • A handwritten welcome note

India edition

Our launch box is built for arrivals from India: vegetarian by default, Jain and vegan on request, and a few familiar things from home for the first evening. Editions for other countries will follow when we open bookings there.
