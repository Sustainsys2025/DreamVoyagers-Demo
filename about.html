About DreamVoyagers

We started with a simple observation: every newcomer to New Zealand faces the same first-week problems, and nobody packages the answers.

Why we exist

Immigration New Zealand lists accommodation, banking, an IRD number, a phone and utilities among the things every newcomer must arrange. Universities publish checklists. Airlines land. And then a nineteen-year-old stands in the arrivals hall with two suitcases and a dead phone.

DreamVoyagers is an arrival concierge. We don't own hotels or shuttles — we coordinate trusted Auckland partners into one plan, with one point of contact, so the first week is calm instead of chaotic. We are part of the ANZ Pharma group, an Auckland business with twenty-plus years in logistics, warehousing and distribution — which is exactly the muscle an arrival service needs behind it.

How we work

  • One plan, one team. Your host, your room, your SIM and your appointments are coordinated by the same small team you message on WhatsApp.
  • Honest about limits. We help with the practical things. We don't give immigration advice and we don't promise what banks or Inland Revenue decide.
  • Start small, learn fast. We are launching in Auckland with a handful of carefully chosen accommodation, transport and food partners, and adding what our first customers actually need.
