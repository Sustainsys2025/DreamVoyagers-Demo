About DreamVoyagers

Every newcomer to New Zealand faces the same first-week problems, and nobody packages the answers. We do.

  • Auckland-based, part of the ANZ Pharma group
  • Launching with arrivals from India
  • Practical help, never immigration advice

Why we exist

Immigration New Zealand lists accommodation, banking, an IRD number, a phone and utilities among the things every newcomer must arrange. Universities publish checklists. Airlines land. And then a nineteen-year-old from Pune stands in the arrivals hall with two suitcases and a dead phone.

DreamVoyagers is an arrival concierge. We don't own hotels or shuttles — we coordinate trusted Auckland partners into one plan, with one point of contact, so the first week is calm instead of chaotic. We are part of the ANZ Pharma group, an Auckland business with twenty-plus years in logistics, warehousing and distribution — the muscle an arrival service needs behind it.

Why India first

India is New Zealand's second-largest source of international students and one of its largest sources of skilled migrants. Many of our own team made that journey. We are starting where we know the questions best, and will open to other countries once the Auckland operation is proven.

How we work

  • One plan, one team. Your host, room, SIM and appointments are coordinated by the same small team you message on WhatsApp.
  • Honest about limits. We help with practical things. We don't give immigration advice and don't promise what banks or Inland Revenue decide.
  • Start small, learn fast. We are launching in Auckland with a handful of carefully chosen accommodation, transport and food partners.
