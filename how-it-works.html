How it works

From booking to your first week in Auckland — what happens, when, and who does it.

Pick a package and book

Choose a package, add a Welcome Box if you like, and tell us who is travelling, when they land and what they eat. Parents can be the booker and the student the traveller.

Pay securely by card

You are taken to Stripe's secure checkout. Only once Stripe confirms the payment do we mark your booking as paid and email your confirmation — never before.

We confirm each piece

Our team books your host, room, SIM, food pack and appointments with our Auckland partners. Each confirmation triggers an email, so you watch the plan fill in.

Pre-arrival pack

Three days before you fly: host's name and photo, meeting point, what to carry in hand luggage, emergency numbers and your day-by-day plan.

Landing day

Your host tracks your flight and is at the arrivals gate with a sign. Straight to your accommodation; SIM activated; first-night food in the room.

Your first week

Bank appointment, IRD application, transport card, groceries, orientation. One WhatsApp number for anything — from “which bus?” to “I feel lost.”

Photo slot: arrivals hall

What happens if something changes?

  • Flight delayed or changed: message us the new flight number. We re-time the pickup free up to 24 hours before.
  • Visa delayed: move your booking to a new date once at no charge.
  • Need to cancel: full refund less a small admin fee if more than 14 days out. See cancellation & refunds.
  • Dietary or accessibility needs: tell us in the booking form; we adjust the food pack, the room and the vehicle.

Ready when you are

Booking takes about ten minutes.

Book my arrival

Your first week in New Zealand, taken care of. An arrival and settlement service for international students, skilled migrants and families landing in Auckland.

DreamVoyagers provides practical arrival assistance. We are not licensed immigration advisers and do not give immigration, legal, tax or financial advice. Bank accounts are approved by banks and IRD numbers are issued by Inland Revenue; we help you prepare and attend, we cannot guarantee outcomes.
