What's in the box

Straight talk about what we can and can't do

Trust is the whole product. So here is what the fine print says, in plain English.

  • We book, prepare and attend your bank appointment. The bank decides whether to open the account.
  • We help you apply for an IRD number and explain each step. Inland Revenue issues it.
  • We don't give immigration advice. We are not licensed advisers, and we will say so if you ask.
  • Your money is protected. Card payments go through Stripe; we never see or store your card details.
