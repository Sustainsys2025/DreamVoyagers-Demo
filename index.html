Straight talk about what we can and can't do

We book, prepare and attend. Banks and Inland Revenue make the decisions — and we will always say so.

Every flight met

We track your flight. Delayed six hours in Singapore? Your host is still there with your name on a sign.

Your money is protected

Card payments go through Stripe; we never see your card. Full refund if you cancel 14 or more days before arrival.

Real people, 7 days

One WhatsApp number, answered by hosts who made this same journey themselves.

No immigration advice

We are not licensed immigration advisers and will not pretend to be. Practical help only, said plainly.
